# Yield entry ownership after measured localization recovery

`relocalization.preserve_yield_intersections: true` preserves fixed-route yield duties through a measured, stopped map reset. It defaults to false/omitted; a recovery policy with yield controls needs this explicit option. Supply a surveyed `localization_map`, a straight forward route and synchronous XY LiDAR under the [regional recovery contract](regional-relocalization.md). Stop signs and traffic signals need their own preservation flags. The native dynamic chassis calibration can use local route geometry because a straight route keeps its original surveyed arc coordinates.

```json
{
  "relocalization": {"preserve_yield_intersections": true}
}
```

An entry already committed before the loss remains owned when the measured front is at or beyond its stop line. It stays `Proceeding` inside the crossing and becomes `Passed` when the measured rear clears the exit. A previously owned entry now ahead is rearmed. Uncommitted clear permission never survives a reset. The measured pose cannot establish an entry permission that was never observed.

The reset clears every blocker diagnostic, clear timer and scan count, and advances the scan watermark to at least the reset time. An acquisition at or before the reset cannot credit a new dwell. Every uncommitted entry needs a new full second of healthy, aligned clear scans, with distinct acquisitions, at most 0.15 s accepted age and no acquisition gap over 0.2 s. The collision planner continues inside an already committed crossing; retaining entry ownership does not bypass sensor or localization braking.

Admission requires absolute estimated speed at most 0.05 m/s, measured center distance plus body radius plus 0.2 m strictly inside the original corridor, and heading error below 0.2 rad. Every unowned/rearmed entry needs at least 0.5 m front margin. An invalid admission latches `InvalidIntersectionRecovery` and -6 m/s² braking through later fresh scans and GNSS. The fault also blocks new signal passage, stop-sign dwell and yield commitments. A later valid sensor feed cannot pardon an unserved crossing.

The [independent evaluator](../scripts/check_yield_relocalization.py) reconstructs ownership from preceding acquisitions and transitions; reported phases, commitments, timers and reset diagnostics do not seed it. A separate distance-based circular occupancy calculation scores actual ego/priority occupancy and the unchanged two-second temporal separation. Continuous circular collision sweeps, actual waiting-line margin and full-interval localization scores remain separate checks. Unit mutation controls reject forged permission, reset ownership, fault braking, scan credit and physical priority summaries.
